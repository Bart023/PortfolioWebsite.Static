Bart van der Burg

Full stack .NET developer

Nederland

I am a dedicated software engineer with a structured and pragmatic approach. I gain a lot of energy from solving complex problems and working on challenging projects.

Principles such as DRY, KISS, Less is More, and SOLID are second nature to me, because I believe in code that is logical, clear, and maintainable. I also value a good balance between quality and quantity, since good software stands or falls with a solid foundation. For example, I find it important to regularly take a step back rather than only moving forward, and to review code with a critical eye so it can be continuously improved and its maintainability, scalability, and quality remain high.

In addition to my technical side, I am also creatively minded and enjoy contributing to the user experience and visual design of a product. Whether it’s front-end, back-end, or anything in between, I always strive for a well-balanced harmony between technology and design.
