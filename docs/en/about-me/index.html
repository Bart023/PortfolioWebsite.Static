Bart van der Burg

Full stack .NET software engineer

Amsterdam - Nederland

I am a driven software engineer with a structured and results-oriented approach. I gain energy from solving complex problems and working on technically challenging projects.

Principles such as DRY, KISS, Less is More, and SOLID form the foundation of my way of working. I believe in code that is logical, clean, and maintainable, and I place great value on a healthy balance between quality and delivery. Good software is not just about moving forward, but also about deliberately taking a step back and evaluating with a critical eye. By continuously refining code, actively sharing knowledge, and leveraging feedback, maintainability, scalability, and overall quality remain high.

In addition to software development, I enjoy working on DevOps-related topics. For me, development does not stop at writing code, setting up and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and reliable, well-designed (cloud) infrastructure are equally important.

I am also creatively minded and enjoy contributing to the user experience and visual direction of a product. Whether it involves front-end, back-end, infrastructure, or everything in between, I always strive for a thoughtful balance between technology, usability, and design.
